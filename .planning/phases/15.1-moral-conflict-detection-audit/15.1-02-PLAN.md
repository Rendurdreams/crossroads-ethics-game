Players never see conflict messages for the precisely-targeted triggers at the moment they matter most.

Purpose: The condition-specific triggers fixed in Plan 01 only work when called with a complete history entry. This plan wires them to work at round-close in the player view.

Output: Play.jsx computes hasMoralConflict with full choice context. ConsequenceReveal.jsx receives full context via props and passes it through correctly.
</objective>
